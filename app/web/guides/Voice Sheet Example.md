## Alex

- **Gender and age:** (proposal) male, early thirties.
- **Origin / accent:** (proposal) neutral American English with a plain, practical delivery.
- **Register and timbre:** (proposal) compact midrange baritone, a little rough at the edges.
- **Tempo and rhythm:** (proposal) economical, with a short pause before a dry observation.
- **Speaking personality, 0–5:** sarcasm 2, warmth 3, energy 2, formality 1, deadpan 5, vulnerability 1.
- **Humor:** reports an absurd inconvenience as if it were a routine maintenance problem.
- **Relationships:** with Morgan, quietly warm and willing to be corrected; with strangers, concise.
- **Under pressure:** practical and precise; humor becomes shorter or disappears.
- **Never:** smug superiority, a booming action-hero voice or an audible wink at every joke.
- **Voice description:** A man in his early thirties with a clear, slightly rough American baritone.
  Practical and understated, with short phrases and dry warmth.
- **Delivery directions:** State the absurd as calmly as an ordinary fact. Let the listener find the joke.
- **New preview:**
  I checked the route twice before we left, which seemed sensible at the time. The map is perfectly clear.
  Unfortunately, the road has apparently decided to disagree with it. We can wait here and debate the
  matter, or take the bridge before the rain starts.
- **Second preview:**
  We do not need to solve the whole thing tonight. We need a dry room, a working phone and enough light
  to see the stairs. Once we have those, we can decide what comes next. For now, stay close and let me
  check the door before anyone goes through.

## Morgan

- **Gender and age:** (proposal) female, mid-thirties.
- **Origin / accent:** (proposal) clear, educated American English without an exaggerated regional mark.
- **Register and timbre:** (proposal) bright middle-register mezzo, clean and precise.
- **Tempo and rhythm:** (proposal) even, with questions given room to land.
- **Speaking personality, 0–5:** sarcasm 1, warmth 4, energy 3, formality 3, deadpan 3, vulnerability 2.
- **Humor:** asks a reasonable question and allows the contradiction to become apparent.
- **Relationships:** with Alex, friendly precision; with strangers, patient and politely direct.
- **Under pressure:** organizes information and keeps the phrasing clear; tension appears in breathing.
- **Never:** icy superiority, theatrical indignation or a constant sarcastic sneer.
- **Voice description:** A woman in her mid-thirties with a bright, clear mezzo and educated American
  diction. Attentive and quietly amused, with precise but natural phrasing.
- **Delivery directions:** Keep the courtesy sincere. Allow the final word of a question to carry the irony.
- **New preview:**

  We have a door, certainly, and it opens. It simply opens into a cupboard. I am happy to count that as
  progress, provided nobody asks me to call it an exit.

## Important contrasts

- **Alex / Morgan:** compact, rough-edged and economical versus clear, bright and evenly articulated.
- **Alex / Narrator:** a more clipped conversational rhythm versus flowing narrative phrasing.
- **Morgan / Narrator:** different register and timbre, with questions and interpersonal reactions reserved
  for Morgan rather than performed by the narrator throughout the prose.
